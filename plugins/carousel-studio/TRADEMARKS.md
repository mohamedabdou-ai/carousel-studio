## What you can do without asking

- Use, study, modify and redistribute the code under the Apache License 2.0.
- Fork the project and publish your fork, under a different name (see the next section).
- Say truthfully what your work is: "based on Carousel Studio", "a fork of Carousel Studio", "compatible with Carousel Studio". Link to the original repository when you do.
- Write or talk about Carousel Studio, review it and teach it.
- Mention that you made a post with Carousel Studio. You do not have to. Nothing the Studio exports carries its name or its author's name, and you own everything you create with it.

## What a fork must change

1. **Rename it.** Give the plugin a new `name` in `plugin.json`, a new marketplace `name` and listing in `marketplace.json`, and a new display name in the README and the Studio screens. Do not use "Carousel Studio", «كاروسيل ستوديو», or a name that can be mistaken for them (adding a word, a number or a suffix is not enough).
2. **Replace the logo and the icon.** Do not ship the project logo in a fork.
3. **Keep the legal notices.** Apache-2.0 requires you to keep the `LICENSE` and the attributions in `NOTICE`, and to mark the files you changed.
4. **Do not imply endorsement.** Your fork is yours; do not suggest that the original author reviewed, supports or is responsible for it.
5. **Do not use "Claude" or "Anthropic" as part of your fork's name.** Those are Anthropic's marks, and this project is independent of Anthropic.
