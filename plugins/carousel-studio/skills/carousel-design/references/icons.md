# Icons
397 icons, exact names (tb- Tabler, cs- custom, the rest Lucide). Search by meaning: python scripts/icon_search.py --query "<arabic or english>".
Rules
1. Choose for the slide's idea, not its first noun. Each icon once per deck, never on adjacent slides.
2. Warning or negation: triangle-alert or circle-x, or the concept icon with the slash modifier.
3. Heritage and occasion icons (party-popper amphora castle cs-camel cs-dallah cs-dunes cs-fanous cs-finjan cs-khatam cs-qalam dome moon-star mosque pyramid tb-building-arch tb-building-burj-al-arab tb-building-mosque tb-horse) only when the slide's own words name them; never as decoration, never beside a person icon.
4. xl and hero sizes: detail l or m icons or an object; detail h icons stay lg or below.
5. Faceless people only; no brand logos. Never a typed glyph or emoji in place of an icon.
