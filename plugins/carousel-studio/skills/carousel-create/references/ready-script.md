# The creator's own script

Use this for a finished script: numbered slides, sections split by `---`, «ده السكريبت» / «السكريبت جاهز». First router row wins even when speed is requested. Their words are the script; no writers' room, new hooks or audience panel. Their own Reel/post starts here at step 2 after the director prepares `script.md` from owned material. Read this page, brand lookup and dialect only as needed; specialists read their own briefs. Do not open schemas/engine/script source. Art designs, QA exports and vetoes, and a fresh Creative Director reviews the team's work before the creator sees the design.

## Steps
1. **Brand.** Find `brand.json` (`brand-lookup.md`). Take dialect/digits, memory, standing `ext.styleChoice` and `ext.publish`. Keep the creator's hook. Ready scripts from the creator may have 2 to 10 slides including the cover, the QA hard minimum; 4 to 6 is complete. Team-written guided/fast carousels require 4 to 10. Below 4 stays a QA FIX, never a board error. Never pad or drop their words to reach a count; offer a split/merge when required.
2. **Save the script** exactly as pasted to `<post>/script.md` (the post folder is `post-YYYYMMDD-NNNN` in the workspace or next to the brand). Parse this file directly; never write a `script.parse.md` copy. Role labels, Markdown spans and backticks are formatting; their text stays. A quoted prompt stays copy. Standalone source credits are reported as mentions, not rendered text; tool names inside a sentence stay in the copy. Links become sources automatically and keep their visible labels, with tracking parameters removed and the originating slides linked; this does not fetch or verify a page.
3. **Structure:** `python scripts/script_to_spec.py --script <post>/script.md --brand brand.json --out <post> --architecture <one word>`, plus `--style <preset>` when the user named one. A long slide runs on at its own paragraph or sentence end, every word kept and in order, within the platform's slide budget (10). This includes the slide-2 and closing-slide splits. A script already over the limit is kept in full: offer the reported neighbouring merge candidates, shortest pair first; never merge or truncate on your own. When the user says the script is final («كما هو», «زي ما هو», «من غير تغيير», "as is"), add `--no-split` and record allowed length overrides in step 5 without asking again. The architecture is one of `news, story, case-study, listicle, steps, myth-fact, opinion, comparison, framework, results, offer, lead-magnet`; pick it from the topic in one thought. The command prints its decisions, the missing fill and fresh QA counts, even before the caption pack exists. Exit 2 can mean a saved spec with open BLOCKs; a `cs-error` means the write was refused.
4. **Fill (copywriter role pass):** write `<post>/fill.json` with only missing bridges and caption fields, in the post's dialect; `script.md` stays verbatim:
   - `bridges`: one per slide from 2 to the last but one, at most 8 words; it opens the next slide and never repeats its title.
   - `emphasis` (optional): one keyword per slide that is already in its title or headline, e.g. `{"s04": "توقيت"}`.
   - `caption`: `firstLine` (at most 125 characters, a reason to tap «more», not the cover again), `keywordLine` (the words people search for), `body` (the whole caption 300 to 900 characters: the payoff in short lines, no new claims, no numbers that are not in the script), `cta` (the same single action as the last slide), `hashtags` (3 to 5, topic first, no tool or author tags). A caption the creator wrote is the caption: split it into these fields word for word, emoji included, and write only a missing field. Hashtags they named and the brand's `ext.alwaysHashtags` come first, inside the five.
   - `perSlide`: one line per slide, at most 100 characters. `alt`: one per slide, in the post's language, saying what the slide says and shows.
   - Existing `edits`, `motion`, `teamLog` and `keep` fields still work in older fill files. For deliberate changes after the first fill, use `spec_edit.py` below. Offer a shorter line or a single action in one line; never apply it without the creator's yes.
5. **Check:** `python scripts/script_to_spec.py --spec <post>/spec.json --fill <post>/fill.json --brand brand.json`. If `SRC1.missing` names the creator's own figures, ask once: «هل تؤكد أن الأرقام التي كتبتها صحيحة وتتحمل مسؤولية مصدرها، أم تريد إضافة مرجع؟». Only after confirmation, run `spec_edit.py source attest` or re-run this step with `--attest-numbers`. This creates a `user` source for the currently unsourced figures; it never replaces an attached reference. New figures require a new confirmation. For a supplied reference use `source add`; a source name without a URL is not verified evidence. For an allowed copy override, ask once and run `spec_edit.py keep C1.hero-words` (or `C2.body-words@s06`). Offer an approved shorter version through `cover`, `text` or `cta`. Technical and non-overridable BLOCKs stay open. Run `python ../arabic-proofread/scripts/ortho_lint.py --spec <post>/spec.json --brand brand.json` for Arabic; fix your own spelling and offer changes to the creator's words. The Arabic editor runs only on request.
   If the script ends on its last item, offer **one** closing line and explain its kind: save, send, question, keyword-dm (with delivery), or follow. Do not add a question or a reply request silently. On approval use `cta`; on «زي ما هو» use `cta --keep`. That records the creator's choice without adding words: `C4.count` and `C5.skeleton` stay in the report marked as their override («تجاوز»), the decision survives every rebuild, and the Studio and the export honour it. Only the creator's own «زي ما هو» does this; never record it for them.
6. **Lock hook and style before design.** Hook is theirs. Director uses the art brief as a read-only shortlist role pass: rank `creator`, `notebook`, `poster` by fit/pre-flight, brand default only a tie-breaker. Show `render.mjs --style-sheet --spec spec.json --brand brand.json --out style-lab`; creator chooses, or «زي ما تشوف» delegates and director states which. A named style or standing `brand.ext.styleChoice` skips only that question. No art-director/visual-artist dispatch or picture generation before this lock. Motion recommendation is provisional; no motion agent/storyboard unless requested. If called, motion designer decides and director writes approved `cover.motion` with `spec_edit.py apply --file approved-motion.json` (`post.cover.motion`).
7. **Art design pass.** Dispatch `cs-art-director` marked «fast lane» with absolute Post folder, Brand file, create/proofread/design Scripts and actual proofread `data/rules/ortho.json`/`copy.json`, locked hook/style, Pass: design, Pen holder: art director, exact May write/Must not. It writes its own `visual-plan.md`, designs every slide including cover, merges only named free blocks with `merge-free.mjs`, runs one render/review sheet. No slide is off limits. External covers include frame-0/source files. While art holds spec pen, director queues changes and applies them with `spec_edit.py` only after its handoff card. After any creator checkpoint, extract latest Studio edits before writing (`extract_spec.py page.html -o spec.json --expect-rev N`); exit 75 means re-read. Review passes never write; findings go to a fix pass of the same role.
   Pictures only if agreed and an image tool is connected, after art sets slide types: dispatch visual artist for `plates.mjs slots`, prompts/manifest; director obtains approval and calls the tool; artist checks every original for letters/digits/logos/watermarks, records dated checks; director runs `plates.mjs apply --dir <post> --brand brand.json` and `check`. Never fit with ad-hoc scripts.
8. **QA, then Creative Director, before handoff.** Dispatch `cs-qa-producer` with all script/brand paths and stable spec: it runs the full export itself with `--brand`, every gate, opens every slide full size and the 130 px `contact-sheet.png`, checks text/brand/crop/AI records/kit, returns BLOCK/FIX with slide ids. BLOCK veto stops the post. Then dispatch `cs-creative-director` fresh: compact final review, only rendered hook at 130 px, flow of split, craft, proof and CTA as placed; Idea, Value, Voice each print `لا تُقيَّم: كلمات المبدع`. Never request rewording; offer a copy issue to creator in one line. An explicit kept ending without CTA is not a defect. `اللجنة: لم تُشغَّل`. REVISE: ≤3 named notes, one loop (fix → QA → review); unresolved vetoes stop delivery.
9. **Creator sees the reviewed Design.** Director writes `director`, `design` and `editor` only if one ran in `spec.ext.teamLog`; no pitch/duel/swipe. Design line names `QA: ok|PASS|FIX|BLOCK` and `المدير الإبداعي: SHIP|REVISE|KILL`. Run `python scripts/board_check.py --stage ready --spec <post>/spec.json`. Rebuild/republish the Studio at the same link; say what to inspect and give the log. After approval: memory (panel fields absent), `hooks.history`, kit, then publishing preference once (`brand.ext.publish`, posting guide). Never assume platform or publishing permission.

## Change a finished spec

Use `python scripts/spec_edit.py --spec <post>/spec.json --brand brand.json COMMAND`. Never patch `spec.json` with Python heredocs, JSON substitutions or other ad-hoc code. `show` is read-only; content/design changes increment `rev`, invalidate the frozen design, validate the schema before writing and print open BLOCK and FIX counts. Use `--format json` for one JSON object and `--expect-rev N` when acting on a previously read revision. Exit 75 on a revision conflict means re-read and retry. For an intentional new slide structure, use `script_to_spec.py --script script.md --out POST --discard-edits` or `spec_edit.py --spec POST/spec.json reset-record`: each backs up `spec.edits.json` as `spec.edits.json.bak` and starts a new record. `reset-record` leaves the spec unchanged; it does not replay the discarded intent. An existing backup is never overwritten: move it aside after reviewing it before another reset. The shared lock is `spec.json.lock`; inspect an abandoned lock before removing it.

```text
show
style notebook
cover --headline "موعد واضح" --sub "خطوة صغيرة" --kicker "دليل عملي"
text s02 --field body --text "النص الذي وافق عليه المنشئ."
text s03 --field bridge --text "والخطوة التالية؟"
source add --url "<source-url>" --label "تقرير النتائج" --slide s02 s03
source attest
alt s02 "وصف الشريحة بعد المراجعة."
caption --field firstLine "سبب واضح لقراءة التفاصيل."
caption --field perSlide --slide s02 "تعليق الشريحة."
caption --field hashtags "#تنظيم" "#عمل"
cta --kind save --title "ارجع إلى الخطوات" --text "احفظ القائمة."
cta --kind keyword-dm --title "دليل الخطوات" --text "اكتب «دليل»." --keyword "دليل" --delivery instant
cta --keep --reason "المنشئ اختار النهاية كما هي"
override post roles.accent=#2468AC
override s02 type.scale.body=48
keep C2.body-words@s06
apply --file approved-design.json
```

`text` accepts `title`, `body` or `bridge`; `alt` and per-slide captions accept a stable id or position. `caption` also accepts `keywordLine`, `body`, `cta`, and `asset` with `--keyword`, `--delivery` and optional `--description`. A cover edit updates the hook and exact cover quotes in captions, per-slide captions and alt text; unrelated descriptions stay. Override values are scalars only; an unknown key reports the valid engine token paths. Sources use `location` for the label (the schema has no `label` field). No reference is fetched. A caption has no `sourceIds` field: attach references to slides.

For approved picture assets, motion, free designs or a Studio URL, `apply --file` accepts `{"post": {"assets": [...], "meta": {"studioUrl": "..."}}, "slides": {"s02": {"media": {...}, "free": {...}}}}`. Write that design file, then let the command validate and record it. It cannot change identities, revisions, approvals, frozen data or QA. Never bypass the command with ad-hoc code. After changing copy on a free slide, have its design updated and rechecked too.

## What survives a re-run

Keep `script.md`, `fill.json` and **`spec.edits.json`** with the post. The edit record is written by the commands; do not hand-edit it. Old fill files require no conversion. On the first rebuild of an older spec, existing sources, creator assets/media, cover lines, captions and design fields are captured in the record. Later rebuilds also capture Studio design choices not already owned by a recorded edit. Managed `plate-*` assets and their media/check records stay in `plates/plates.json`; run `plates.mjs apply` after rebuilding so an old JPEG cannot overwrite a restyled picture.

Every run applies, in order: parsed script (or the current spec for step 2), the retained legacy fill, deliberate edits in their recorded order, then attestation of the unchanged figures. Saved Studio caption and bridge changes update the retained fill before a new partial fill is merged. Changed incoming fields win; unchanged values in a reused fill do not undo the Studio save. Use `spec_edit.py` for an intentional return to an older value. It binds numbers, validates the schema, re-runs QA and applies only permitted copy overrides before replacing the files atomically. Deliberate edits win over an older fill file. The record is written before the spec so a run interrupted between replacements can recover by replay. A fill-only record follows stable slide ids when the structure changes, dropping missing per-slide keys and leaving new captions/alt text to fill. A structure change with deliberate edits is a conflict; review the old intent and use the backup/reset commands above to start again. Setting a CTA clears an earlier `cta --keep` decision and its no-CTA overrides. New content or design edits require design review again.

## In chat
Before the reviewed Design link: what stayed verbatim, any approved split, what art fixed and what to inspect next. Hook/style choice comes before design, never after pictures. Give the actual team log; after design approval deliver the kit and the one manual/tool preference question. Never paste the script or caption back.
