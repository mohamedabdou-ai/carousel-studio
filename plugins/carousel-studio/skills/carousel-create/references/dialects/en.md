# English (en)

**Status:** stable. **Register:** white. **CTA style:** direct. English posts are set left to right (`dir="ltr" lang="en"`) for the whole post; Arabic names keep their script inside it. The Studio chrome and the swipe cue follow the audience's Instagram app language (`arrow-ltr` for an English interface), not the post's language.

The data behind this page is `data/dialects/en.json` (an empty deny list; a warn list of machine-written English; CTA lines).

## How it sounds
- **Plain, specific, brief.** One claim per sentence; a concrete noun, tool or number instead of an adjective.
- **Digits, not words**, on covers and slides: "6 tools", not "six tools". Western digits, Latin punctuation, `…` for the two-beat break.
- **Sentence case** on covers and titles unless the brand's samples use title case. No all-caps shouting, no emoji on slides.
- **Contractions are fine**; a reader who sees "do not" on every line hears a notice.
- **Active voice, second person.** "You can save this" beats "This can be saved".

## Machine-written English to avoid
The data file's warn list and the AI-words list flag the usual tells: delve, game-changer, unlock, unleash, supercharge, revolutionize, elevate, leverage, seamless, cutting-edge, harness the power, a testament to, secret sauce, "in today's fast-paced world", "it's important to note", "dive into", "next level", "you won't believe", "life-changing". Also avoid the stock openers: "Did you know", "Have you ever wondered", "Welcome to", "Here are some tips", "In this post".

Two more tells that are not on a list: a dash in every second sentence, and a rule of three in every list. Vary the rhythm.

## Covers
The cover limits are the same as Arabic: hero at most 6 words, whole cover at most 12, at most 3 lines, the meaning word first, one specific gap left open. Break the two beats on the ellipsis: "6 free tools…\nthat replace a designer".

## Mixed content
- Arabic names, titles and quotes inside an English post stay in Arabic script and are isolated by the engine; do not transliterate them.
- If the audience reads both, do not write half the post in each language. One language per post; a bilingual caption is a different request.

## Caption and hashtags
Same bands as Arabic: 300 to 900 characters, line 1 at most 125 characters and not a copy of the cover, the keyword in sentence 2, at most 5 specific hashtags, one CTA. Alt text in plain English.
