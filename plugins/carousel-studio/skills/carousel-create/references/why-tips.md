# «ليه؟» tips

A short reason next to a decision teaches the user why the plugin works the way it does, and makes them trust the next step. One sentence, in the user's `uiDialect`, at the moment it matters.

## How to use
- **One tip per review point**, as its own line after the message, introduced by the label below. Never two tips in a message, never the same tip twice in a session.
- Guided mode: `angle` and `pick` at review point 1, `script` at review point 2, `design` at review point 3. Use the others (`hooks`, `numbers`, `digits`, `dialect`, `cta`, `caption`, `short`, `slide2`, `bridge`) only when the user asks «ليه؟» about that decision or the decision just changed their mind.
- Fast mode: show only `fast`, once, in the opening line.
- A tip is a reason, not a lecture. No statistics beyond what the research supports (these tips contain none), no promises, and no tip for something you did not do.
- If the user says they do not want tips («من غير ليه»), stop for the rest of the session.
- Label (put it first, then the sentence): eg «ليه؟» · gulf «ليش؟» · levant «ليش؟» · msa-white «لماذا؟» · en "Why?"
- Gulf and Levantine lines are marked **beta**: they have not been reviewed by native writers yet (`nativeReviewed: false`). Keep them as they are; do not "improve" them with local slang.

### en — English
| key | tip |
|---|---|
| angle | We start with the angle because the same topic can be a post nobody remembers or one people save, and the angle is the difference. |
| pick | I picked this one because it is closest to your goal and easiest to back with something real. |
| hooks | Three hooks from three different families, so you compare how they feel, not only the words. |
| script | Review the script in the Studio, not in chat, so the text shows exactly as it will be set. |
| short | A slide with few words is read in seconds, and that is what keeps people swiping. |
| slide2 | Slide 2 is shown again to people who skipped slide 1, so it works as a second cover and pays off first. |
| bridge | Every slide ends on a small open question so the next one gets read. |
| numbers | Every number needs a source: one wrong figure costs the whole post its credibility. |
| digits | Western digits 123, because every numeral on the winning Arabic covers we studied was Western. You can change it. |
| dialect | One dialect per post, so the reader hears one voice. |
| cta | One call to action, after the payoff: two split the reader's attention. |
| caption | Line 1 of the caption gives a new reason to swipe; it does not repeat the cover. |
| design | We design after the words are settled, so the layout does not keep changing. |
| fast | Fast mode is on: I chose the angle myself and opened the Studio straight away. |
