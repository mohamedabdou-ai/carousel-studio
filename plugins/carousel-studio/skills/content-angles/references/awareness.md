# Awareness: who is reading, and what may the cover assume?

Four levels (the `awareness` field of the spec): **unaware**, **problem**, **solution**, **product**. The level decides what the cover may take for granted, which hook families fit, and what the closing action should be. Pick it once per angle and carry it into the spec.

## The four levels
| Level | The reader… | The cover may assume | Families that fit | Architectures | Closing action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **unaware** | does not know there is a problem; usually a non-follower met in the feed | nothing: open with a surprise, a scene or a belief | P4 flip, P5 forward reference, P12 news, P13 imagine, P16 giant number, F20 dilemma, F21 stance, F22 experience; also P9, P10, F17 | myth-fact, story, opinion, news | **send** or follow |
| **problem** | feels the pain, has not found the answer | the pain: name it in their words | P1 list, P3 costly mistake, P6 specific question, P9 cold-open story, P10 identity, F17 reframe, F18 irony, F23 iceberg, S3 loss; also P4, P5 | steps, myth-fact, listicle | **save** |
| **solution** | knows solutions exist, is comparing | the category: skip the "why", go to the choice | P2 vs, P7 sharp-number result, P8 before to after, P11 pack, P14 X without Y, P15 tested and ranked, F19 pre-check, S2 benefit; also P1, P13, S1 | comparison, listicle, framework | **save** |
| **product** | knows your offer, deciding | the offer and the name | S1 offer; also P7, P8, S2 | offer, results, lead-magnet | **keyword-dm** (only with a real asset), follow, question |

Source: the `awareness` field of each family in the hook library of carousel-create (it started from the playbook's mapping and the usual awareness of each family in the hook bank). The first level a family lists is its home level; "also" marks the other levels it may serve. This table is the planning view: when it and the library differ, the library wins.

## Defaults
- **Reach** and **send** goals aim at people who do not follow the account yet: write for **unaware**, with no jargon and no brand name on the cover.
- **Save** goals aim at followers and returning viewers: **problem** or **solution**.
- **Sell** goals: **solution** or **product**; the post is soft-sell and every claim is the user's.
- Use `brand.audience.awareness` when the brand set it; override per angle when the lens says otherwise (a `news` angle is unaware by nature; a `setup` angle is solution).
- Mixed audiences: write for the lower level. A problem-aware reader can read an unaware cover; the reverse loses the unaware reader at once.

## What changes in the copy
- **Unaware:** one scene or belief on the cover; slide 2 shows why it matters in the reader's life. No "you need our…".
- **Problem:** the cost of the problem on slide 2, the fix from slide 3. Name the pain with the reader's words, from `brand.audience.pains`.
- **Solution:** go straight to the comparison; honest limits build trust. Prices and plan names sourced.
- **Product:** proof first (a screenshot, a result with its source), then the offer, then one clear step. No invented scarcity, no guarantees, no testimonials the user did not provide.

## Check
Before showing angles, every angle must have an awareness level and a family that fits it. If the best-scoring angle needs a family that does not fit the level, change the level or the angle; do not force the hook.
