**Write your idea in your dialect. Get an Arabic carousel that's ready to post.**

Carousel Studio is a full writing and design team inside Claude, built for Arabic creators. Start from a one-line idea, notes, an article link, a PDF or a transcript, and get back a carousel with a hook that stops the scroll, slides designed for your topic, a looping video cover, the caption, hashtags and alt text.

## What the team does

- **Pitches two directions**, one safe and one bold, then lets 12 or more hooks compete for the cover.
- **Tests the script on your audience:** three personas cast from your brand say where they would stop, save or keep scrolling.
- **Writes in your dialect and voice:** Egyptian, simple "white" Arabic and English, with Gulf and Levantine in beta.
- **Typesets Arabic correctly:** joined letters, English words and numbers in the right order, one digit system per design. An independent Arabic editor can veto the copy.
- **Designs every slide for the topic** in three styles (Creator, Poster, Notebook), with a 6-second looping MP4 cover whose first frame is the finished design.
- **Checks before you post:** word counts, safe zones, contrast, empty space, headline size at thumbnail scale, and numbers that need a source. Each finding comes with a fix.
- **Remembers your brand:** a short onboarding builds a Brand Book you approve, and the team remembers what you posted, loved and rejected.

## What you get

3:4 PNG slides, 4:5 JPEG crops, the MP4 cover, a PDF, caption, slide captions, alt text and up to five hashtags in one light ZIP. The plugin does not publish itself. After approval it asks once where/how: manual files or your connected tool. It remembers the preference and follows that tool's instructions and limits, on your word each use for named accounts/platforms; no publishing-tool recipe is bundled.

## Install

In the Claude app (desktop or web), open **Customize**, then **Plugins**. Click **Add**, then **Add marketplace**, then **Add from a repository**, and paste `mohamedabdou-ai/carousel-studio`. **Carousel Studio** then shows up in **Discover**: open it and click **Add**. It is saved to your account, so it follows you to the desktop app, the browser, your phone, Cowork and Claude Code. Turn on **Sync automatically** for the repository to get new versions on their own.

From a terminal in Claude Code:

```text
/plugin marketplace add mohamedabdou-ai/carousel-studio
/plugin install carousel-studio@mohamed-abdou
```

## How to use it

Say «ابدأ» (start) to set up your brand, or ask for a carousel right away, in any dialect: «اعملي كاروسيل عن…», «سوّ لي كاروسيل من هالمقال», «بدّي كاروسيل من تفريغ الحلقة هاي», or "make a carousel about…". Say «بسرعة» (quick) for a smaller team and fewer steps. Shortcuts: `/carousel`, `/post`, `/brand`, `/plan`, `/audit`, `/studio`.

The Studio, a page that opens in your browser on phone or computer, lets you edit text in place, switch styles, preview the post in the feed and export.

## What it runs and connects to

**This plugin sends no data.** There is no server, no account, no telemetry and no analytics code.

- Skills and commands run inside Claude. Its scripts (validation, linting, building the Studio page) run in Claude's sandbox, or on your computer in Claude Code.
- The Studio runs in your browser. Exporting the MP4 cover loads one pinned library (`mediabunny` 1.61.0) from `cdn.jsdelivr.net`; nothing from your post is sent. PNG, JPEG, PDF and ZIP exports make no request.
- A source link you give is read with Claude's own tools. Tools you connect yourself (an image generator, Canva, a scheduler) run only after you approve each use.
- The plugin never asks for, uses or stores API keys.
